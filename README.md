# STILL

**Abandoned, Not Forgotten**

**STILL** is a minimalist interactive storytelling experience documenting thirteen silent spacecraft resting across the solar system—from the dusty plains of the Moon and the rust-red deserts of Mars to the cold expanse of interstellar space.

For over six decades, crewed landers, rovers, and deep-space probes departed Earth on one-way journeys. None of them stopped because of failure; they worked until their batteries froze, their solar wings disappeared under dust, or their fuel tanks ran dry. **STILL** preserves their voices, archival photographs, and official NASA/NSSDCA records along an outward journey scaled by real radio light-time delay.

---

## Chapters

### I · The Moon (`1.28 s` light delay)
- **Apollo Lunar Modules (Descent Stages)** — *Six launch pads standing in the lunar silence*
- **Lunar Roving Vehicles** — *Three electric cars parked in Hadley and Taurus-Littrow*
- **ALSEP & Retroreflectors** — *Nuclear seismic stations and passive laser mirrors still in use today*
- **Surveyor 3** — *The only robot on another world ever visited by human beings*

### II · Mars (`12.5 min` light delay)
- **Sojourner** — *The microwave-sized pioneer that taught humanity how to drive on Mars*
- **Spirit & Opportunity** — *Twin ninety-day geologists that explored the red plains for years*
- **Viking 1 & 2** — *Humanity's first eyes on the rocky surface of Mars*
- **Phoenix & InSight** — *Stationary sentinels that touched water ice and listened to marsquakes*
- **Ingenuity** — *The four-pound rotorcraft that flew seventy-two times in thin Martian air*

### III · Deep Space (`Hours` across the dark)
- **Dawn** — *In a silent quarantine orbit around dwarf planet Ceres*
- **NEAR Shoemaker** — *An orbiter never built to land, resting on asteroid 433 Eros*
- **Pioneer 10 & 11** — *Interstellar pathfinders carrying gold-anodized maps toward the stars*
- **Kepler Space Telescope** — *Drifting in solar orbit after revealing a galaxy crowded with worlds*
