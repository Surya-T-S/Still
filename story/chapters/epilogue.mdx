From Apollo 11's Eagle descent stage standing in Tranquility Base to Kepler drifting in an endless solar orbit, every retired spacecraft remains an embassy of human curiosity and engineering endurance. In the airless vacuum of space, metals do not corrode, rubber does not rot, and tire tracks remain undisturbed for millions of years.

They sit in patient silence across the solar system, waiting for the day human hands return to brush the red dust from their solar panels, examine their telemetry memory cores, and read their odometers. "Abandoned" is a word for things discarded without purpose. These machines were planted like seeds across the dark. Now, the journey continues with you.
