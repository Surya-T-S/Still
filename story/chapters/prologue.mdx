For over six decades, robotic probes and crewed landers have departed Earth on one-way journeys. They were not designed to return. Once their batteries depleted, solar panels coated in dust, or fuel tanks ran empty, they remained exactly where their missions concluded.

As you scroll outward through STILL, the radio signal delay from Earth will stretch in real time—from a second and a half at the Moon, to twenty minutes across the Martian desert, to hours in the deep cold beyond the planets. Every machine resting in the dark was an instrument of human curiosity. Listen to what they recorded before their signals faded.
