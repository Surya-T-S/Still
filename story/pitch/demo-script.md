# STILL — 2-Minute Video Demo Script
### NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 · *Abandoned but not Forgotten*
**Speaker:** Nandana (Storyteller)

---

### [0:00 – 0:25] The Hook & Thesis
*(Visual: Opening on the pitch-black Prologue screen. Distance: 0 km. Signal Delay: 0.0 s.)*

**Nandana (Voiceover):**  
"Humans have left nearly two hundred tonnes of hardware on the Moon. More rests on Mars. And some is still coasting in the cold between the stars. 

We often call these machines 'space junk' or 'abandoned.' But abandoned is the wrong word. 

This is **STILL**—a scroll-driven journey through our silent fleet, where every piece of hardware we left behind gets one last moment to tell you what it gave humanity."

---

### [0:25 – 0:55] Act I: The Moon & The Signal Delay
*(Visual: User scrolls down. The HUD pins to the top: Distance: 384,400 km. Signal Delay: 1.28 s. Palette warms to silver.)*

**Nandana (Voiceover):**  
"The spine of STILL is **The Signal Delay**. As you travel outward, the speed-of-light delay grows. On the Moon, radio waves take 1.3 seconds. 

Here we meet the Apollo descent stages—the launch pads that stayed behind so humans could come home. We meet the Moon Buggies, parked forever with their tire tracks still visible from NASA's Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter. 

And ALSEP: Apollo's laser reflectors. You can click 'Ping the Moon' right now—our laser fires, and two and a half seconds later, the echo returns. We still use them to prove the Moon is drifting 3.8 centimeters away from us every single year."

---

### [0:55 – 1:25] Act II: Mars & The Lone Explorers
*(Visual: Scroll crosses into Mars. The HUD jumps: Distance: 225 Million km. Signal Delay: 12.5 minutes. Accent shifts to rust red.)*

**Nandana (Voiceover):**  
"Cross into Mars, and the delay jumps to minutes. The loneliness deepens. 

You hear from Sojourner, the microwave-sized rover that opened the path. You meet Spirit and Opportunity—planned for 90 days, but driving for nearly fifteen years across ancient lakebeds until dust darkened the sky. 

And we keep it honest: we bust the viral internet myth. Opportunity never said *'My battery is low and it's getting dark'*—that was a journalist's poetic summary. STILL separates what is imagined from what is real."

---

### [1:25 – 1:45] Act III: Deep Space & The Messengers
*(Visual: Scroll enters Deep Space. HUD stretches to hours. Accent turns faint ice-blue. Line art of Pioneer 10 and Kepler.)*

**Nandana (Voiceover):**  
"In Deep Space, the signal delay stretches into hours. NEAR Shoemaker, a spacecraft never built to land, that kissed an asteroid at four miles per hour and whispered back data for two weeks. 

Pioneer 10, drifting toward the star Aldebaran carrying a golden map of Earth. And Kepler, which stared at 150,000 stars to prove that our galaxy is crowded with worlds."

---

### [1:45 – 2:00] The Epilogue & Closing
*(Visual: Epilogue screen. User types a one-line message, selects Tranquility Base, and downloads a shareable orbital postcard.)*

**Nandana (Voiceover):**  
"At the end of STILL, visitors don't just read history—they leave a keepsake of their own, generating an orbital postcard with the real signal delay to their message.

Every number is verified against NASA's NSSDCA catalog. The machines may have stopped talking. But they never stopped mattering.

They are still here. Now, so are you."
